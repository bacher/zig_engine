# Capture and build with blueprints

## Intent

Let a player save a valid arrangement of blocks as a reusable blueprint and apply it to a prepared site. Validate the completed design when creating the blueprint, so assembling many buildings later needs only constant-time local structural checks per placed block.

The first runtime version is implemented; the hybrid-admission follow-up below awaits external specification review before implementation. Requirements distinguish user-confirmed behavior, **DERIVED** correctness/performance consequences, and **ADOPTED DEFAULTS** chosen to complete routine UI details. Defaults may be revised without implying that the user explicitly selected them. No gameplay question remains blocking this iteration; concrete resource/latency limits must be selected and measured during implementation. Q20 defines one current ruleset and world-save version gates for incompatible changes. Use **blueprint** in code and documentation.

## Context

**Approved hybrid design, pending external review and runtime implementation (2026-11-06):** R39/R44/R50–R52 and R58–R65 specify whole-chunk protection limited to the proposed construction box, potentially stale exterior structural evidence, and current publication checks. The current runtime and existing validation reports still describe version-checked admission with a retained mutation FIFO. T31 gates implementation on external review; T27–T30 track the runtime follow-up. Earlier checked tasks and measurements do not verify it.

The voxel app has a one-block player inventory, timed manual placement/extraction, debug spectator tools, a main-thread streamed world cache, and an authoritative `WorldDataService` worker. Client and service currently run ordinary structural validation, including potentially affected constructions. Persistent saves include terrain, construction metadata, roads, block timers, player identity, inventory, blueprint definitions, and active construction progress.

The selected capture design uses the player's current client view, including already-applied optimistic edits, then validates detached contents in isolation. The previous service-owned source-snapshot design is retained under [Rejected alternative: WorldDataService-owned capture](#rejected-alternative-worlddataservice-owned-capture) as decision history. It is superseded and is not a second implementation target. Authoritative destination checks still apply when applying a blueprint.

## Requirements

### Definition, ownership, and scope

- R1: A blueprint is an immutable arrangement of logical block types at integer 3D cell offsets. Its completed normalized arrangement must satisfy construction rules under the foundation contract in R15–R18.
- R2: A private blueprint belongs to the player who created/received it and is stored in that world's save, keyed by player identity; no cross-world profile is introduced. A shared blueprint is available to all players on its multiplayer server. Capture and future interactive planning produce private blueprints by default; future progression rewards produce shared blueprints by default. Private-to-shared conversion is a future operation.
- R3: This iteration implements capture from existing structures and application/assembly. Interactive blueprint design, rewards, sharing, and multiplayer remain future acquisition/distribution paths.

### Capture and input

- R4: Z enters capture only with empty player hands. Otherwise show an on-screen error, for example `Cannot enter blueprint capture while holding a block.` Capture does not consume a block or mutate terrain.
- R5: While capturing, display `Blueprint capture` in the upper-right corner. A held low-poly blueprint model is deferred. **ADOPTED DEFAULT:** keep the existing empty hands visible this iteration.
- R6: Capture proceeds through three fresh Mouse1/E presses: anchor cell, XY extent, then Z extent. These are immediate presses rather than the normal timed place/mine interaction. Movement and looking remain available during selection; ordinary block actions are suppressed.
- R7: The first target preserves ordinary putting-target selection, including the adjacent placement cell for a physical hit and the full planned cell for a pending miniature, with capture reach extended to **30 metres**, inclusive. First-anchor hover and confirmation use the same target query and the existing distance from the camera to the candidate cube's nearest vertex, using its nearest wrapped X image; this is separate from the XY/height raw-contact distance from player feet. Ordinary placement/mining reach stays four metres. The first fresh Mouse1/E press commits that currently eligible anchor. Initially the confirmed inclusive box is 1×1×1. During XY selection, normally intersect the camera's view ray with a fixed horizontal XY plane through the anchor cell and floor its X/Y coordinates. **CONFIRMED fallback:** when camera storage Z lies in the anchor block's slab `[anchor.z, anchor.z + 1)`, instead target the first actual physical solid block along the ray, skipping miniatures, and take the adjacent `Hit.placement` cell's X/Y while forcing endpoint Z to anchor Z. This fallback uses bounded traversal sufficient for the extent range, without the ordinary four-metre interaction reach. Its exact physical contact Q remains separate from the placement cell; do not floor Q to choose fallback X/Y. In either branch choose the shorter wrapped X interval under Q25 and keep one-cell height. At the second click, freeze X/Y and a vertical plane through the accepted exact fractional plane/physical contact Q, with its normal taken from that click's horizontal camera yaw. During height selection use absolute world/grid Z from the fixed plane: `endpointZ = floor(rawHeightHit.z)`, without an offset or anchor-relative baseline. XY selection stays one cell high; entering height selection may immediately jump above or below the anchor to the actual contact Q height. Do not reevaluate the XY targeting policy after this click. The normal XY-plane branch and height selection work in open air without a voxel hit; the XY fallback requires a valid physical hit and adjacent placement cell. In all extent paths the raw contact/intersection must be within **30 metres of the player's current feet position**, inclusive, under Q12's metric, before projection, quantization, or wrapping. Reevaluate range as the player moves and before confirmation. Invalid or beyond-range targeting retains the last valid box and disables confirmation. The limit applies to the current contact/intersection; previously confirmed bounds remain fixed, and moving the player allows boxes larger than 30 metres within R54's horizontal size cap. World bounds and configured resource budgets still apply. Allow extension above or below the anchor. Dimensions are `abs(endpoint - anchor) + 1` after lifting the shorter X interval into the anchor's signed unwrapped frame, with no zero-size box or off-by-one loss at either end. Plane positions, quantization, and invalid-target handling follow Q12.
- R8: While capture awaits its first anchor, immediately draw a 1×1×1 frame at the currently eligible cursor target using R7's 30-metre anchor query; hide it when no eligible target exists. This hovered frame is transient and unconfirmed: it does not set the draft anchor/bounds or advance selection. Throughout selection and after confirmation, draw the box with the frame described in Context. The three stages produce a cell, a one-cell-high rectangle, and a volume respectively. **CONFIRMED occlusion treatment:** the selected capture frame, including its unconfirmed anchor frame, remains faintly visible behind world geometry. Draw its occluded portions first with depth comparison `.greater`, faint blending, and depth writes disabled, then draw the normal opaque overlay with `.less_equal` and depth writes enabled so exposed rails retain their ordinary appearance. Reuse the same uploaded instances: the extra instanced draw covers only the selected frame's twelve rails, 144 triangles. Miniatures, full-size application previews, and other active sites' frames receive no occluded-frame draw. The frame/selection is noncolliding and does not alter voxel occupancy.
- R9: Before accepting the third press or starting capture-specific loading/copy, verify the raw dimensions under R54. For an eligible box, begin client-owned preparation/validation and show loading or validating status. Obtain all selected cells in full block form, freeze the player's local logical view under R41, and validate that detached snapshot on a bounded helper. Enter/name entry remains disabled until the matching successful helper result arrives. Only a valid candidate enables Enter and the upper-right hint `Press Enter to create a blueprint.` Highlight invalid included blocks in red and explain the rejection. Empty selection, unknown source data, cancelled jobs, and stale results cannot be accepted as valid. The successful result certifies the frozen snapshot, not a continuing live view of its source.
- R10: Enter opens the name dialog only for a valid candidate; a subsequent Enter in that dialog confirms the name and saves. Require a nonempty name of at most 100 characters. Confirming saves a private blueprint; failure must report an error rather than claim success. Character counting, duplicates, and save durability are specified by Q8/Q9.
- R11: With the chooser closed, before any anchor/first block is selected, including while an unconfirmed hovered frame is visible, Escape leaves capture and opens the ordinary game menu; Resume returns to ordinary play. Once an anchor has been selected, Escape cancels the entire draft capture, including from the name dialog before confirmation: discard box, candidate, diagnostics, and pending name, then stay in capture awaiting a new anchor without opening the menu. A subsequent fresh Escape from that anchor stage, with the chooser closed, leaves capture and opens the menu. Invalidate the selection generation immediately, release capture-specific chunk retention, and cancel/release the associated client helper job/candidate; late results cannot restore it. Z exits capture and discards that state, except while entering a name: Z must be available as text and must not toggle the mode. After final save submission, apply Q9's save-owned payload semantics rather than promising draft cancellation.
- R12: Before final save confirmation, capture state is temporary and must not enter the saved library, autosave records, or gameplay simulation. The main thread owns UI, source capture, and candidate lifecycle; a bounded helper may temporarily own the detached validation input/output under Q16. Before Save, WorldDataService participates only through ordinary chunk loading/subscriptions, with no blueprint preparation job or candidate. Cancel/release unconfirmed jobs/candidates on reset, exit, or owner disconnection; never mistake one for a saved blueprint. Name entry requires a successful matching helper result. Confirming the name transfers the checked definition to the service for publication under Q9; disk durability is a separate state. After Save submission, cancellation of tool UI must not free or silently cancel the definition now owned by the save operation.
- R13: Capture the player's current merged logical view: real included cells with already-applied local pending edits, plus expected types from unfinished blueprint miniatures. Read the existing predicted cache rather than replaying pending commands a second time. A miniature means the same planned block as a completed real cell. Capturing does not modify or complete its source instance.
- R14: The saved definition must have all empty outer layers removed on all six sides and occupied cells translated to nonnegative offsets from the compact minimum corner. Keep internal empty cells. Raw and compact dimensions must satisfy R54; compaction does not rescue an oversized raw selection. Normalization, compaction, and validation may occur during preparation before name entry; save exactly the certified immutable contents rather than trimming or changing them afterward. Empty contents have no valid compact form.

### Full validation and portability

- R15: Validate the complete normalized arrangement in isolation. Source blocks outside the selected box supply no structural support. Treat all included cells as construction, including cells copied from generated terrain; generated-terrain exemptions cannot certify a blueprint.
- R16: Let `z_min` be the globally lowest occupied layer after compaction. Assume a full stable platform beneath **each occupied cell at `z_min`**, not beneath every column's first occupied cell at a higher Z and not beneath every empty cell in the bounding rectangle. The virtual foundation is the union of these platforms; other external cells provide no support.
- R17: A platform beneath cell `(x,y,z)` consists of 27 qualifying cells at offsets `dx,dy ∈ {-1,0,1}`, `dz ∈ {-3,-2,-1}`. Use the current shared stable-platform material predicate. Platforms reset height counters; they do not remove the ordinary height/span limits above that reset. The ordinary isolated-deeprock shortcut does not replace this blueprint-specific 3×3×3 requirement.
- R18: Apply normal structural limits to the normalized isolated arrangement above the virtual foundation, including internal platforms and the existing fixed support window, after correcting ordinary independent-support routes under R38. Keep existing direct-support material rules: crushed_stone alone does not support rebar/reinforced_concrete; a qualifying platform or legal lateral route can still support them. R47 changes captured materials, not these rules. During application preparation verify full real platforms and composed compatibility in the observed destination input under R39. R62 protects box-chunk contents; exterior input may become stale. At publication R59 requires a known actual nonempty cell directly below every globally lowest occupied target: an uninterrupted protected observation proves this where covered, and external supports are read again immediately before commit. Do not repeat 3×3×3 platform checks. Later external extraction follows R37 without permanent foundation locks.
- R19: First normalize **every included** `stone → crushed_stone` and `grass/packed_dirt → dirt`, then validate the resulting isolated arrangement. R47 permits one narrowly scoped concrete-support fallback; otherwise reject invalid normalized contents and highlight the affected source cells. Preserve occupied-cell geometry apart from R14's empty-outer-layer compaction; do not remove cells, add supports, substitute other materials, or relax structural limits. A narrow seven-cell stone-only column without an intervening platform reset still becomes invalid crushed stone and rejects capture. This iteration supports dirt, sand, crushed stone, tree trunk, planks, rebar, reinforced concrete, and solid country road. Reject leaves, natural resource types, clay, deeprock, water, snow, adaptive roads, and unknown custom blocks rather than dropping them. Dirt/packed dirt/grass are one logical fulfillment material, so normal aging does not undo a completed target.
- R20: **DERIVED correctness safeguard:** a blueprint must be assemblable with the cheap rule in R28–R30. Certify creation-time isolated reachability from the assumed bottom foundations; the initial design must not need another site's targets. Reject unreachable cells if found. Use the same directed support predicate as runtime R28, including the reinforced pair and natural-rock XY support for crushed stone. The compiler may fuse this check with structural certification if equivalence is proved for the allowed materials; do not add runtime searches. After application, ordinary world changes may introduce cross-instance dependencies under R52; creation-time certification is not a permanent independent-completion guarantee.

### Selection, preview, and application

- R21: B opens blueprint selection in ordinary play or while capture awaits its first anchor, including while an unconfirmed frame is hovered or after a draft reset. Opening the chooser over unanchored capture keeps capture mode, its anchor-stage selection, and selection generation unchanged. Cancel/Escape/B closes only the chooser and restores that capture state; choosing a definition exits capture into normal placement preview. A chooser opened from ordinary play returns there on dismissal. B after an anchor has been selected remains ignored outside name entry. Ctrl+B toggles SSAO blur separately, subject to modal input blocking. F has no blueprint-tool binding. With no accessible blueprints, show `You do not have any blueprints yet.` and leave the current mode unchanged. Capture and placement preview must not run simultaneously.
- R22: Preview the complete building at the cursor with normal full-size block appearance. Preview blocks have no collision, support, mining, inventory, or world-mutation effects. R rotates 90° clockwise viewed from +Z; bare R must not consume Ctrl+R. Anchor/rotation details and cancellation are Q7.
- R23: A fresh Mouse1/E press submits application at the preview's current origin/rotation only if the destination is admissible. Freeze that transform while admission is pending and suppress duplicate tool submissions. **ADOPTED DEFAULT:** a submitted command owns its request independently of tool UI; Escape/B/Q may dismiss the tool but cannot cancel that command or an applied building. Show pending status and deliver its eventual success/failure even after dismissal. A failed apply keeps preview available for adjustment only if that preview remains open. Clear held/queued tool actions on entering/exiting tool states so a chooser click or old placement hold cannot apply it. Check the rotated footprint against R54 before large preparation. Atomic publication must satisfy R59 using uninterrupted protected target evidence and current external-bottom, bounds/ownership, overlap, resource, and index-integrity checks. R44/R58 permit stale exterior structural input. Failure leaves no partial instance or changed terrain. Releasing protection always invalidates preparation; a retained storage wait preserves the submitted request under R64, while non-storage preparation expiry terminates it under R65.
- R24: An unfinished blueprint's box must not overlap another unfinished blueprint's box. Every nonempty blueprint target cell must be physically empty when applying, including cells occupied by the correct material: existing blocks never count as prefulfilled targets. Blueprint-empty cells may contain existing real blocks; leave them untouched. Such contents must be compatible with the composed completed world under R39; do not require all cavities empty. Preview must explain application failures.
- R25: After application, show the compact rotated box with the same frame. Initially every target displays a matching block miniature, approximately one-quarter linear block size, centered within its full cell and apparently floating. Miniatures have no collision, voxel occlusion, or physical terrain occupancy and never count as real support for cheap fulfillment. Ordinary extraction validation treats them as completed logical blocks under R37.
- R26: A miniature must still be an interaction target: use the full planned cell for blueprint placement targeting, not only the miniature's visible geometry. Ordinary player reach and player-body collision checks remain applicable to the real block placement.

### Assembly and completion

- R27: A pending target accepts only its blueprint's required logical material. Successful placement consumes the normal carried block and replaces the miniature with a real block atomically; rejected placement preserves both inventory and miniature. Dirt aging, prediction, service rejection, and reload must not duplicate or lose fulfillment.
- R28: Structural approval for a blueprint-directed placement examines at most five immediate **physical** world neighbors: one below and four face neighbors in the same XY plane. If the below cell has any actual real block (`material != none`, never a miniature), accept without ordinary material-support, height/span/platform searches. If below is physically empty, require at least one eligible already-real XY neighbor. The directed predicate is matching logical material, either member of the rebar/reinforced_concrete pair for a reinforced target, or `neighbor.isNaturalRock()` for a crushed_stone target. The existing natural-rock family is stone, metal_ore, limestone, coal, and deeprock. This covers ordinary crushed-bridge support without changing ordinary rules, permitting natural-rock targets, or substituting any support material for the required target item. Count eligible real neighbors outside the instance too, including fulfilled targets of another site. Miniatures of any site never provide physical support. Unknown data is not proof of occupancy or emptiness.
- R29: The five-neighbor bound is a hard requirement for the structural approval path, on both client and authority, including dependent checks invoked indirectly. Do not run ordinary recursive support searches, dependent collection/scans, or per-instance/per-building scans for each blueprint placement. Pay for completed-design validation during blueprint creation; platform/destination admission is paid once per application. **CONFIRMED implementation clarification:** blueprint fills enqueue coalesced adaptive-road updates using bounded chunk metadata/index work; they do not run the ordinary road planner. Process proposed road shape changes separately, rejecting unsafe changes before commit. Shape-only plans check each changed road and any real/planned road directly above it against the complete final plan in the completed logical world; ordinary material edits retain broad affected-construction validation. Process a bounded batch per service turn, initially 16 work units counting seed processing and entry completion or requeue, and yield on dependency, source-hold, or error waits. Preserve in-progress pass cursors across metadata revisions; completed dirty passes move to the queue tail for another pass. A small delay in road shape updates is accepted. Every durable capture persists the full set of unfinished typed road/chunk obligations with current terrain, progress, inventory, and global state. Do not wait for the road queue to empty or hold later mutations solely for its drain. Preserve selected command fences, source holds, bounded retries, and normal dependency/read/compaction conditions. Reserve marks before accepted effects, persist completed results and clears together, restore saved keys as fresh current-world passes before readiness, and preserve valid saved road ownership. See [persisted unfinished world work](deferred-world-work.md) for the shared representation and acceptance criteria.
- R30: Temporary violations of ordinary construction rules during assembly are intentional, including internal support and compatibility with surrounding structures. The exception covers partially built structures and already completed structures affected by unfinished construction, including buildings outside the active box. A six-cell beam may be assembled from one supported end while its other endpoint is still a miniature. Do not require legal ordinary physical intermediate states or re-run full validation at completion. R50–R51 also explicitly permit lasting ordinary-rule violations introduced by stale destination admission; a short observation window does not imply automatic repair or eventual ordinary validity.
- R31: Forbid extraction of **any real block inside the entire active box**, including preexisting nontarget cells. Enforce the restriction for player actions, debug tools, and simulation/service mutation paths. Outside the box, use ordinary extraction against the completed logical world under R37, without special foundation locks. New ordinary placements in blueprint-empty cells follow R40.
- R32: When every target is fulfilled, remove all miniatures, the frame, and instance-specific restrictions. Maintain a remaining-target counter so identifying completion is O(1). The completed real blocks then participate in ordinary construction/removal rules, with other active instances still represented in the completed reader under R37/R40. Finishing this instance alone does not promise its ordinary physical validity while nearby construction remains unfinished; R50–R51 apply. Do not add a final structural or neighboring-instance scan.
- R33: **DERIVED persistence requirement:** persist saved blueprint definitions in the owning world's per-player library, and persist unfinished applied instances including their fulfillment state, consistently with real blocks and inventory. Unfinished buildings must survive restart; drafts must not. Persisted instances must retain their immutable definition even if library-management features are added later.
- R34: Future cancellation of an applied instance removes only its frame, miniatures, and instance restrictions; already placed real blocks remain. Cancellation UI/commands are not implemented here. A temporarily invalid remaining building after that future cancellation is an explicitly accepted gameplay compromise, including deliberate player exploitation. Future cancellation and its effects on remaining or surrounding structures are excluded from the current invariant in R50 and its consequence in R51; that behavior requires separate design.
- R35: All world-changing actors and mutation paths use the same authoritative blueprint placement contract and the ordinary completed-world checks in R50, with R44/R58–R59's explicit stale-Apply exception. This iteration supplies the contract for future NPC construction; it does not implement NPC scheduling, transport, or material delivery.
- R36: **DERIVED progress invariant:** while an instance is active, forbid destructive/type/shape-changing overwrites of fulfilled targets, not just extraction. Permit certified equivalent transitions such as approved dirt aging. No timer, simulation, debug, or indirect write may leave a fulfilled bit representing a missing/different block. This protects progress bookkeeping; it is not a permanent foundation/support certificate.
- R37: For ordinary extraction outside active boxes, treat pending miniatures as already built blocks of their expected logical types. Validate the hypothetical removal exactly as if every active construction were complete, using ordinary structural rules, the same fixed support windows, and all affected real **and planned** constructions. Retain physical mining/occupancy/item checks so miniatures themselves cannot be mined. Reject a removal that invalidates the logical completed world; allow one that preserves it even if it dismantles part of an original 3×3×3 platform. Do not add a separate prohibition on foundation extraction or repeatedly require the original platform cube. This full check belongs to ordinary extraction, never cheap blueprint fulfillment.
- R38: Change ordinary construction rules before certifying blueprints: distinguish a block below being present from its eligibility to supply support. In particular, planks over dirt/packed dirt/grass may use a legal lateral plank support route even when the earth below is not a stable platform. The nonqualifying below block contributes no support and cannot veto an independently valid lateral route. A failed direct-support attempt must not suppress a valid existing lateral-support mechanism; materials still retain their own span, height, footprint, and crushed-column/bridge restrictions. Trunks gain no new lateral-support mechanism, and an unsupported or unknown below block does not itself become a foundation. Apply the same corrected validator to ordinary placement, existing-structure/removal checks, isolated blueprint certification, and composed admission.
- R39: Once per preparation attempt, fully check compatibility of the complete new blueprint with the observed real destination and other active sites' completed logical blocks. Validate new targets and potentially affected existing real/planned constructions under corrected ordinary rules. Reject invalid observed input atomically with diagnostics; preserve harmless cavity contents. This remains necessary because joining a six-cell crushed-stone blueprint column to an existing one-cell crushed-stone bridge above can create a forbidden seven-cell run. Under R44/R58 box-chunk evidence is protected while exterior input need not describe the publication-time world. Exterior changes alone do not trigger full structural revalidation; R59 remains required. A retry after protection release takes new copies and validates a new attempt under R64. Full structural work never moves into each fulfillment.
- R40: For ordinary placements in blueprint-empty cells or near active sites, require the proposed new block to pass target validation in **both** physical terrain and the completed logical view, then protect affected real/planned dependents in the completed view. Both target checks include the same hypothetical new block; the completed reader also overlays pending targets of every active site. Reject the ordinary edit atomically if any check fails. Checking completed dependents alone is insufficient: dependent enumeration excludes the edit cell and cannot establish the new block's completed-world validity. Physical approval remains necessary so ordinary construction cannot obtain real support from miniatures. Enforce the same contract for client previews and authoritative admission. Blueprint-directed fulfillment is a separate operation and remains a structural no-op in the completed view with the five-neighbor bound.
- R41: The main thread freezes the player's current selected logical contents into an owned immutable snapshot once every selected cell is available. Capture need not match one authoritative world revision and does not wait for pending-edit acknowledgment. Subsequent source updates, prediction rebases, or rejection of a captured optimistic edit do not change/invalidate the snapshot; its isolated contents must still validate. The client retains the checked candidate until Save or cancellation. Save publishes **that same checked snapshot**, without recapturing the live box or retaining source chunks during naming. Validation and save payloads use local blueprint coordinates and require no source world location. The certificate applies to isolated contents under the running game's single current ruleset, not source revisions or a separately selected historical rules version.
- R42: Blueprint selection and name dialogs block gameplay movement/look input and terrain tools but **do not pause the world**. Player gravity/collision, simulation, NPC/service edits, block timers, streaming, preparation/helper replies, and persistence continue. Resuming input must clear queued/held tool actions so closing a dialog cannot accidentally place or mine.
- R43: Support approximately **100×100×32 selected cells** as the large first-iteration scenario, with most blueprints expected to be smaller. This is a required workload target where the world dimensions permit it, rather than an exact maximum dimension. R54 separately fixes the hard horizontal size cap, including for sparse designs; smaller worlds obey that cap. Additional technical limits derive from explicit memory/work/queue budgets and must accommodate the reference workload in sufficiently large worlds, including worst source-chunk alignment and wrapping-X cases. No benchmark result or fixed latency claim is implied.
- R44: **CONFIRMED hybrid destination admission:** prepare applications asynchronously from bounded owned immutable copies. Before the first box terrain/index copy, protect exactly R62's full-box chunk set; do not protect the surrounding structural dependency halo. Conflicting terrain/index writes wait, while eligible unprotected installations/extractions, fills, and background changes progress. The helper fully validates observed platforms/composition; R58 permits stale exterior evidence. At atomic publication use R59's current conditions and R60's prepared whole-index swaps. Exterior terrain/planned-overlay revisions alone do not invalidate preparation. Preserve producer/save ordering under R61 and protection lifetime and expiry/fair reacquisition under R64/R65. Atomic apply describes visibility/commit, without requiring a current exterior snapshot or a full-building validation on the sole service thread.
- R45: **DERIVED memory requirement:** bound helper/compiler caches and diagnostic storage independently from the raw snapshot. Do not retain a full inline detailed failure record per successful validated cell across an entire large blueprint. Use compact cached states/evidence with separate failure details or a bounded cache lifecycle, while preserving shared structural semantics and useful diagnostics. Measure actual type sizes, allocator capacity/peaks, and cancellation cleanup.
- R46: **DERIVED throughput requirement:** account for mutation, metadata, response cloning, meshing, and persistence costs separately from the five-neighbor structural bound. Support coalesced/batched authority replies or revision-safe deltas for high-rate fulfillment rather than require a full 32 KiB chunk copy per target unconditionally. NPC scheduling remains out of scope; the fulfillment/protocol design must not prevent batching. Keep ordinary player acknowledgments and reconciliation responsive and correct.
- R47: **CONFIRMED concrete-support fallback, replacing the withdrawn general support change:** keep the ordinary direct-support rules unchanged. If initial normalization is valid, retain it without extra substitutions. Otherwise, only when the **original logical snapshot** already contains rebar or reinforced_concrete, allow `crushed_stone → reinforced_concrete` at a support directly below an invalid reinforced cell whose crushed-stone support is incompatible and which has no other legal support route. An existing qualifying platform or valid lateral route needs no replacement. The supporting crushed stone may be original or stone-derived. Continue down the same necessary support chain when a replacement concrete cell itself has this conflict, stopping at a legal support route; each replacement must be traceable to an original reinforced cell's conflict. Original reinforced material elsewhere is insufficient to repair an unrelated crushed column. The guard is computed before conversion, including miniature logical types; newly introduced concrete cannot establish eligibility. Fully validate the final arrangement and R20 assemblability under unchanged limits before certifying it. Change only the captured definition, never source terrain. Q21 specifies the cascade examples and implementation constraints.
- R48: **CONFIRMED compatibility policy:** the game uses one current construction/gameplay logic, shared by ordinary validation and the blueprint compiler; no world or blueprint selects a historical ruleset. For a sufficiently incompatible gameplay change, advance the world save's `current_write_version` and raise `minimum_readable_version` to exclude incompatible old worlds. Their entire saves, including blueprint libraries and unfinished instances, are rejected through the existing base/WAL version gates before gameplay starts. Do not add a per-blueprint rules version, old-rule execution, automatic recertification, or migration policy. Compatible older file encodings may remain readable when they map to the same current logic.
- R49: **CONFIRMED fallback failure diagnostics:** if the resulting support path exceeds the existing mineral-height limit of 20 levels, reject capture with an explicit error stating the mineral height and limit, for example `Blueprint mineral height 21 exceeds the limit of 20.`, and red source-cell highlights; do not offer Enter/name entry. A continuous vertical reinforced_concrete/rebar chain reaching level 21 without a reset or other valid route is invalid. Prioritize the lower included cells of the offending support chain, especially substituted supports, rather than highlighting only the upper cell where the limit was detected. Preserve these lower-cell diagnostics when limiting highlight storage and map them to the original selection. ### Horizontal size and wrapped target uniqueness

- Q25: How do we choose an unambiguous X interval and prevent wrapped target shortcuts?
  A25: **CONFIRMED, replacing the earlier full-circumference cap:** require both inclusive horizontal dimensions of the raw selection and compact definition to be at most `L = min(world_block_size_x / 2 - 1, world_block_size_y)`, regardless of occupancy or volume. World X sizes are even under the existing layout contract. After the raw plane/physical contact passes the current-player 30-metre check, derive the endpoint X/Y through the active Q12 path, compute the shortest wrapped X displacement `dx` from the anchor cell, and lift the endpoint to `anchor.x + dx` in the recipe's signed unwrapped frame. The X size is `abs(dx) + 1`; Y uses its ordinary inclusive extent. Reject an exact half-period tie and any size above L. Thus the raw selection is the untrimmed short-arc box, not accumulated travel around the world. Once XY is confirmed, preserve that interval and the vertical stage's plane; later movement does not reselect the arc. An already formed oversized raw box or definition rejects before capture-specific loading/copy/publication; do not refold it or trim empty layers to bypass the guard. Check the scalar invariant in the compiler, Save, and application using current world dimensions; the helper may receive immutable L without source coordinates or live-world access.

  **DERIVED geometry/locality:** with X period W and accepted X cell count S, any two targets have local X separation at most `S - 1 <= W/2 - 2`. Their unique shortest wrapped displacement therefore equals their local displacement, and distinct well-formed targets cannot alias. The complementary arc between opposite end cells is at least `W - (S - 1) >= W/2 + 2`; with the current minimum `W = 64`, it is at least 34 cells, beyond the ordinary root-centered support radius 28. One support search cannot use that outside arc to reach the opposite end. All quarter-turns preserve these target properties by swapping the two bounded dimensions. Foundation halos retain signed local coordinates; their enlarged extent is not an input to shortest-arc box selection. The formula permits a Y-limited size equal to the full Y extent, subject to actual bounds/platform checks; the default 65536×32768 horizontal world still gives `L = 32767`. This guarantees a short target interval, not independence from external blocks, shared supports, or other sites; several sites may still form a larger ring. Preserve normal wrapped world reads, composed/affected validation, finite destination bounds, platforms, overlap checks, and the isolated compiler's nonperiodic local geometry. Enforce the size/interval conditions with O(1) checked arithmetic, without a per-target world-alias set or per-fill checks; ordinary definition well-formedness remains required.

## Constraints

- GPU operations, tool UI, and source copying remain on the main thread; authoritative real-world mutations and saved blueprint publication remain service-owned. Before Save, use only generic source-chunk subscriptions through WorldDataService. Never autosave a client capture job, candidate, diagnostics, or unfinished name.
- The main-thread copy reads the already-predicted cache plus locally known planned targets at one local snapshot point. Capture does not wait for authoritative pending-edit resolution or enforce cross-chunk authority revisions. Keep all selected chunks in full block mode during preparation; available display geometry or retained stale mesh-mode block data does not prove readiness.
- Run heavy pure compilation/validation on a bounded helper over detached immutable selected-cell data, without live world pointers or source coordinates. Keep the helper independent of WorldDataService's mutable source state. Preserve generation-safe results, bounded queue/memory, and cancellation/shutdown cleanup. **Deferred; do not implement in this iteration.** During review, a stronger progress guarantee was considered: each active instance could fill all its remaining targets using its own targets and actual world blocks, without first fulfilling another instance's miniatures. Batch edits, wrapped coordinates, logical material equivalence, prediction/rollback, and persistence/index reconstruction would all need coherent handling. Keep the selected instance fixed for an entire target evaluation, including recursive support paths, both bridge sides, platform cells, and diagonal probes. Apply the same hypothetical edit throughout a check. Cache reuse must respect the selected view and relevant terrain/planned-index revisions.

The intended purpose is to prevent a site's validation from relying on another site's pending targets, while still allowing support from any actual real blocks. This approach is considered more viable than maintaining a support graph because it reuses ordinary validation and existing spatial indexes without graph preprocessing, component labels, or root counters. That is a design preference, not a measured performance claim. Repeated checks in different views and reduced cache sharing may add ordinary-edit work; future design should retain the existing bounded support windows and affected-region filtering.

Before implementation, define the subjects checked in each view, the application/exterior-mutation admission points, and the argument that the approved cheap predicate preserves independent completion. Checking every real block in every view would falsely reject intentionally incomplete real portions of foreign sites. A foreign fulfillment changes another instance's view and may temporarily make it ordinary-rule-invalid. Continuous validity of every per-instance view is not a promised invariant; R30/R50's accepted temporary physical invalidity remains in force.

These views supplement the all-sites-completed reader: individually acceptable views do not prove union compatibility. Ordinary R37/R40 checks remain; R39/R50 have the explicit stale-admission exception in R58. This deferred alternative must not be read as reinstating a strict current-world Apply invariant. The preference adds no current requirement, acceptance criterion, persistent field, or implementation task, and no recursive searches, per-instance scans, or extra checks to the five-neighbor fulfillment path or O(1) completion.

### Risks

Correcting support eligibility removes false rejections such as side-supported planks over dirt, but not merged crushed-stone runs or sand below water/snow; composed admission remains required. Completed-world checks need planned-dependent indexes and complete material filtering. Client capture needs full-block residency, a measured main-thread copy, bounded helper/cache memory, and cancellation/shutdown cleanup. The approved hybrid follow-up depends on guarding every write into P, including cross-chunk cleanup/road/timer effects, while permitting stale exterior evidence. It retains current occupancy/support/accounting through protected evidence and final checks, prepared index swaps, exact save fences and bounded protection lifetime. Releasing protection invalidates a retained candidate and requires fresh preparation. Existing measurements do not establish its hold duration, observation age or throughput. Capturing a pending edit that is later rejected is intentional player-view semantics. A small raw snapshot does not bound validator-cache, mesh, or response-copy costs. The supported size and no-pause dialog behavior are fixed; actual performance/resource settings require implementation measurements. Adaptive-road capture is deferred, while nearby existing custom-road side effects and indirect writes require the explicit T16 audit. Product details are specified or have explicit adopted defaults; Q20 closes rule-change compatibility through world-save version gates instead of historical rules or migrations.
