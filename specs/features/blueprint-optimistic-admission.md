# Allow optimistic blueprint admission without preparation locks

## Intent

Explore allowing independent world work to continue while a player applies a blueprint, including writes inside the destination input. Prepare from owned, potentially inconsistent observations and enforce current occupancy, ownership and bookkeeping at atomic publication. Accept a bounded period of stale platform evidence as an explicit gameplay tradeoff; measure the scheduling benefit and publication cost before choosing an admission design.

## Context

This is an independent alternative to the full-input-protection proposal in [PR #48](https://github.com/bacher/zig_engine/pull/48). Neither alternative is globally selected, and neither depends on the other. This proposal starts directly from main at `c0ecb6c` after PR #51–#53; it does not require merging #48. Implementation is deferred until baseline measurements, external review and an explicit owner choice between the alternatives.

The [existing blueprint specification](blueprints.md) remains the source for capture, material normalization, immutable certified definitions, size limits, UI ownership, current fulfillment and persistence behavior. This proposal changes destination admission only. Its requirements describe a possible future runtime, not the FIFO/revision-checked admission currently implemented on main.

PR #51 established monotone empty-cell additions, PR #52 removed Apply's old-dependent checks, and PR #53 established portable current-rule certification under the stone-platform restriction. Main already checks physical targets and full real platforms without structural target searches. The [certificate specification](stone-platform-certified-admission.md) and [validation report](../../docs/validation/blueprint-certified-admission.md) describe this delivered baseline. Certified blueprint fulfillment retains at most five physical support probes.

Let N be the number of nonempty targets, B the number in the compact definition's globally lowest occupied layer, P every canonical whole chunk intersecting the rotated compact box, and K the number of chunks in P. P includes empty/cavity-only chunks and complete shared index records. D is P union the canonical chunks covering that box expanded by one cell in X/Y and three cells downward (`minZ-3..maxZ`), with wrapping X, finite Y/Z clipping and deduplication. D describes copied input; this alternative does not protect it against mutations.

The recorded resident 100×100×32 helper benchmark at one origin measured roughly 3.3–3.5 ms for sparse and 27–28 ms for dense definitions after PR #53. It had D=P=32 chunks; it did not exercise additional platform-only chunks. It excluded cold loading/generation, service scheduling, journal/source waits, final preflight/publication, persistence, client meshing and rendering. These results establish neither this proposal's benefit nor #48's end-to-end behavior. Existing index/publication construction scans the entire compact cell array, including air, and clones shared P-index contents; its cost also depends on box volume and unrelated existing entries, not only N.

## Requirements

- R1: Keep this proposal and [PR #48](https://github.com/bacher/zig_engine/pull/48) mutually alternative and independent. Preserve main's existing runtime until measurements, external review and owner selection authorize implementation. This document does not select or implement either model.
- R2: Apply remains player-initiated, with at most one active admission helper/preparation. NPC simulation assembles published instances and does not initiate Apply. Retained commands and helper capacity remain bounded; use the sole service worker for authoritative world mutation.
- R3: Copy complete D into owned immutable buffers, preserving P sources through cavities, the exact rotated XY±1/downward3 envelope, bounds, chunk budgets and canonical wrapped-X identities. Individual copies are authoritative at their copy time but need not describe one world revision. Helpers never traverse mutable live terrain, indexes or instance containers.
- R4: Add no Apply-specific chunk protection or global mutation FIFO barrier during preparation. Eligible independent ordinary edits, fills, timers, roads, batches, completion and cleanup may progress inside P, in D outside P and outside D, subject to their ordinary producer/resource/persistence gates. Copied-source revision or global overlay-epoch changes alone do not reject or restart admission. Residency needed for copying must not become a retained mutation lock.
- R5: Once per preparation attempt, verify physical target emptiness, immutability and full qualifying real 3×3×3 platforms under every occupied target in the globally lowest layer, using the owned observed input. Unknown cells and miniatures cannot qualify. Reuse only trusted immutable current-rule compiler certificates or definitions restored from compatible saves; omit structural target searches and old-dependent collection. Retain definition, rotation and horizontal-size checks from the existing specification.
- R6: Permit terrain/platform observations from different times, including combinations that never coexisted in a single authoritative revision. Roughly 1–2 seconds of age is the intended allowance, not a fixed selected timeout or completion promise. Define and measure a finite configurable freshness limit from the earliest destination terrain/index copy through actual publication, including copying, helper, scheduling, preflight and journal waits. Later copies, progress, polling and helper completion do not reset that clock.
- R7: Immediately before atomic publication, check every nonempty target against current authoritative terrain: it must be known, physically empty and mutable. Matching existing material also conflicts; create no prefulfilled targets. Check current whole active-box nonoverlap, including blueprint-empty areas, plus finite bounds, identity/ownership, definition availability, IDs, resource/capacity and inventory/progress conditions. Check actual current recipient identities and payload ownership, not merely their count. No unknown destination state may authorize publication.
- R8: In the same current publication boundary, require a known actual real block (`material != none`) immediately below EVERY occupied target in the compact definition's globally lowest layer. Any real material qualifies under the existing cheap fulfillment rule; unknown cells and miniatures do not. Do not extend this test to the first occupied cell of each higher-starting column. Do not repeat full 3×3×3 platforms, material-support, height/span checks or structural target/dependent searches at publication.
- R9: Final target and bottom checks cost O(N+B), separately from overlap, recipient/resource checks and index integration. All current checks must remain true at the atomic commit. Yielding a scan and later assuming earlier cells remain current is insufficient. Before runtime implementation, demonstrate a bounded, measured mechanism for these checks and current-index integration on the sole writer without retained admission locks or a preparation FIFO barrier; an unresolved mechanism is an implementation gate, not a claim of constant-time publication.
- R10: Integrate additions into CURRENT live P indexes and current instance/progress accounting. Never overwrite shared records with stale whole-record snapshots, resurrect fulfilled miniatures or completed membership, lose newly added entries, or restore retired instances. Independent fulfillment/completion/cleanup may have changed those records during preparation. Helper-built additions or other unpublished data are not authority for unrelated live state. No live-index merge may require another structural validation pass.
- R11: Prepare/reserve bounded fallible publication resources before changing observable state. Reconcile preflight with current conditions at the final boundary; independent intervening writes cannot invalidate an assumed reservation or recipient. Commit the new instance, indexes, journal and response effects atomically, or reject without any partial terrain, inventory, progress or site changes. Preserve response order, idempotency and durable terrain/inventory/progress consistency. Measure allocation, integration, commit and disposal separately; no O(1) live merge is assumed.
- R12: Preserve each material/inventory producer's required order, contiguous request/checkpoint resolution, player-checkpoint eligibility, atomic transfers, bounded queues and existing dependency/read/backpressure gates while allowing eligible independent requests past retained Apply/blocked commands. Nonblocking preparation does not promise progress for requests that fail independent gates, require parallel live-world writers, or remove the cost of final atomic work.
- R13: Preserve the exact required-command membership selected by each Save. A later bypassed command cannot satisfy an unresolved earlier member. Once selected members are terminal and existing capture-readiness conditions permit, Save captures coherent current authoritative state: independent later submissions committed before capture may be included, and post-capture changes are excluded. Add no global post-fence mutation hold solely to exclude those later independent commands.
- R14: Keep admission buffers/request ownership separate from persistence `source_holds`, save jobs and captured buffers. Existing persistence source holds may independently defer writes and must retain their ordinary release points. Apply must neither reuse them as an admission-region lock nor release another owner's holds. UI dismissal does not cancel submitted operation ownership or allow late results to reopen dismissed tools.
- R15: On non-storage freshness expiry, discard the candidate and terminate Apply once with `preparation timed out; try again`, resolving its command and selected save-fence membership once. Do not automatically recopy/retry because of changed revisions or expiry. A manual retry is a new command; an open preview remains usable and a dismissed one is not reopened. Save/Retry, autosave/coalescing, commit-progress wakes and late helpers cannot revive the terminal request.
- R16: Actual journal/disk wait or storage failure retains the submitted request identity and existing save intent/fence and bounded storage retry policy, but discards its candidate and observed contents. Every permitted recovery takes fresh owned copies and rechecks from current data without replenishing storage retry allowance. If freshness expires while Apply is explicitly retained for that actual storage reason, retain the request under this storage policy; an unrelated persistence writer alone does not qualify. A recovered attempt has a fresh observation clock and the same terminal non-storage expiry rule. Do not add an unbounded automatic refresh/retry loop.
- R17: Rejection, discarded attempts, expiry, shutdown and cancellation invalidate late helper publication. Release attempt ownership exactly once without waiting synchronously for a gated helper; keep its in-use buffers alive until it finishes or relinquishes ownership. Preserve bounded single-helper capacity so invalidation cannot spawn overlapping unbounded work. Unpublished candidates and observation clocks are not persisted or restored; published sites retain ordinary save/replay ownership.
- R18: Accept that stale full-platform observations plus the narrower current bottom test can publish an ordinary-rule-invalid completed arrangement, and that the violation may persist after every miniature is filled. There is no promise that freshness expiry or later fulfillment repairs it. This is an explicit exception for optimistic Apply to the ordinary completed-world validity invariant, not permission for stale occupancy, bookkeeping or missing current real bottom support. Add no automatic repair, last-fill structural scan or new persisted stability flag.
- R19: Preserve ordinary edit contracts: empty-cell insertions validate the new target in physical/completed views, removals/non-additive effects retain affected real/planned dependent checks, and finite additions preserve already-valid existing roots under the monotone batch argument. These operations do not repair or certify pre-existing invalid roots, including invalid targets previously admitted by this proposal. Fulfillment remains a logical no-op in the completed view and uses at most five physical support probes with no ordinary validator or site-wide scan.
- R20: Initial publication retains a cheap structural assembly sequence: the immutable compiler reachability certificate and current real support below every globally lowest target provide the starting roots, assuming required materials/data and no further external mutations. This establishes initial cheap-rule assemblability, not ordinary validity of the destination. After later edits, global completion-order and eventual physical-validity arguments apply only where their ordinary-valid completed-arrangement premises hold; this model does not establish those premises universally. Independent completion of each site and NPC scheduling remain deferred.
- R21: Before choosing or implementing either alternative, measure main with concurrent published sites and player Apply: independent-command delay, accepted/durable fill throughput, full Apply duration, stale rejections and queue/backlog recovery. Cover resident/cold inputs, different chunk alignments with D outside P, sparse/dense workloads, journal/source waits and storage recovery. Separate helper/copy work, O(N+B) current checks, integration, allocations, commit/disposal and client/storage costs. Shared baseline helper timings alone cannot justify either alternative's benefit.
- R22: Record an externally reviewed implementation argument and measurement plan for final atomic checks/live-index integration, expiry/retry ownership, producer/save ordering and stale-composition consequences before runtime changes. If bounded publication cannot support the existing workload without introducing retained locks, a FIFO barrier or a new gameplay tradeoff, return that choice for owner review rather than silently changing this contract.

## Clarifications

- Q1: Does this proposal supersede full-input protection in PR #48?
  A1: No. These are independent alternatives for later comparison and owner choice. #48 protects all D and swaps prepared P records; this proposal leaves D mutable, repeats current target/bottom checks and integrates with current P records. Neither PR depends on the other.
- Q2: What does nonblocking preparation promise?
  A2: No Apply-specific retained admission lock or global preparation FIFO barrier. Independent work can progress even inside D. Producer order, persistence source holds, missing data and resource gates still apply, and final atomic work consumes service-worker time.
- Q3: Can the observed full platform be missing or nonqualifying at publication?
  A3: Yes, within the finite freshness limit, provided the observed preparation passed and every globally lowest target still has any known real block directly below. An unsupported observed combination may never have existed coherently. Lasting ordinary invalidity is an accepted consequence; occupancy and live bookkeeping remain strict.
- Q4: Are higher-starting columns new bottom roots for the final test?
  A4: No. B contains only targets in the definition's single globally lowest occupied layer. Higher targets use the compiler's rooted cheap-support traversal for the initial assembly argument.
- Q5: Is the exact timeout already selected?
  A5: No. Roughly 1–2 seconds states the tolerated observation scale. The finite configurable limit and measured expiry behavior remain tuning work. The R15 terminal non-storage outcome and R16 retained actual-storage outcome prevent automatic retry churn.
- Q6: Is an atomic O(N+B) check and current-index integration algorithm already established?
  A6: No. Their cost is explicit, and the bounded mechanism/proof is an implementation gate under R9/R22. This document does not hide service stalls behind a constant-time publication claim or prescribe an unverified merge algorithm.
- Q7: Which choices are still pending?
  A7: Owner selection between the alternatives, informed by baseline throughput/stall measurements and external review. Exact freshness tuning and final-publication mechanics require evidence before implementation; no further capture or fulfillment product change is proposed.

## Constraints

- Keep the existing approximately 100×100×32 supported workload where world dimensions permit, horizontal-size/wrapped-coordinate restrictions, immutable certificate trust and current world-save compatibility gates. No new per-blueprint rules version, migration or load recertification is introduced.
- Bound copied input, helper caches, proposed additions, current-index integration working memory, response payloads, retained requests and journal reservations. Account for old/new records and late-helper buffers coexisting. Repeated conflicts or expiry must not create O(unbounded retries) retained memory or compute.
- Final authoritative work remains serial and atomic. O(N+B) probes and potentially size-dependent integration can stall other commands while executing; their measured duration and the proposed finite work/memory budgets are required evidence. This proposal does not claim sustained-write starvation is solved or arbitrary workloads always admit.
- Full-D protection avoids stale target/platform evidence and retains prepared O(K) record swaps, but defers writes into all protected input chunks. This proposal permits those writes during preparation but pays current O(N+B) checks and current-index integration, accepts stale-platform invalidity, and can reject after more preparation work. Measure both costs without treating helper-only timing as a comparison.
- No project spec was present at drafting. The existing blueprint, construction, architecture and persistence documents describe main unless and until a chosen follow-up updates them.

## Out of Scope

- Implementing either alternative, selecting the winner, or changing the full-D proposal in PR #48.
- Changing capture/normalization, material rules, five-neighbor fulfillment, NPC scheduling, per-site independent completion, multiplayer, sharing or applied-site cancellation.
- Automatic structural repair, final-building certification, runtime reachability/support graphs, permanent foundation locks or a universal ordinary-validity guarantee after stale admission.
- Parallel world writers, multiple admission helpers, removal of independent persistence holds, or an unmeasured constant-time live-index merge.

## Plan

### Approach

This is a comparison proposal; every runtime step below is conditional on explicit selection. First measure the existing main baseline under R21 and review both specifications. In parallel with review, describe and bound the final current-check/current-index mechanism without implementing the admission model. If the mechanism cannot meet this contract at the supported workload, present the evidence and resulting choice to the owner.

If selected, retain the existing certified helper checks over owned D copies while removing Apply-specific revision invalidation/FIFO retention. Keep copies at their own observation times; build immutable new-site data, never an authoritative stale replacement for unrelated live records. Allow independent work under ordinary producer/source/resource gates. Apply the finite freshness lifecycle and actual-storage recovery rules.

At publication, reconcile fallible resources with current authority, execute the required current target/bottom/global checks, and integrate new entries into live P indexes under the established bounded atomic mechanism. Reject coherently on any failure. Demonstrate interleavings with fills, completion and cleanup in the same chunks, and save fences with independent later commits. Keep permanent structural consequences separate from live bookkeeping correctness in the review and validation report.

### Affected Components

- `src/voxel_app/blueprint_admission_region.zig` and `blueprint_admission.zig`: owned reduced-envelope input, trusted physical/platform checks and observation timing.
- `src/voxel_app/world_data_service.zig`: eligible bypass, current admission checks, expiry/storage/request ownership and publication scheduling.
- `src/voxel_app/blueprint_runtime.zig`: integration with current shared indexes and completion/cleanup ownership.
- `src/voxel_app/blueprint_service_tests.zig`, `blueprint_benchmark.zig` and persistence acceptance fixtures: deterministic interleavings, allocation failures and independent-command measurements.
- Construction, architecture, persistence and blueprint validation documentation: update only if this alternative is selected and verified.

### Data Model Changes

No persistent format change is proposed. Attempt identity, earliest-copy time and bounded unpublished admission state are volatile. Exact live-index integration structures remain an implementation gate; stale copied records are not publishable authority.

### API Changes

Preserve Apply ownership, atomic result/idempotency and save/final-save identity. Return a single preparation-timeout failure on terminal freshness expiry; do not let late results restore dismissed UI. Any necessary internal response/preflight change must preserve authority commit order and current recipients.

### Risks

Helper speedups may leave too little preparation stall to justify the live-index complexity or stale-validity tradeoff. Final scans/integration can become the new service stall. Mixed-time observations can admit a platform that never existed coherently; a short age bound limits observation duration, not structural damage duration. Frequent destination changes can cause final rejection or integration cost even without revision retries. Exact fence/producer semantics, storage-wake ownership and late-helper memory safety must be established independently of structural tolerance.
